Card / Glassmorphism Styles
bg-card · 0.55 opacity
Primary card surface. Used for week nav, session wrapper, stats. backdrop-filter: blur(40px).
bg-card-solid · 0.75 opacity
Stronger card. Used for modals, bottom sheets, session header. More opaque than base card.
bg-elevated · 0.7 opacity
Elevated surface. Lightest glass tier. Used for top-layer floating elements.
surface-inset
Inner inset surface. Used inside cards for nested groups, exercise body sections.
Border radii: --radius-sm: 14px · --radius-md: 20px · --radius-lg: 24px
Button Variants
☀️
Primary: white pill, dark text — used inside dark save bar / toast context.
Accent: blue fill (#0A84FF) — CTA actions, confirm, save.
Secondary: frosted 12% white — cancel, less prominent actions.
Danger: red fill — destructive actions. Icon circle: 44×44, glass backdrop.
